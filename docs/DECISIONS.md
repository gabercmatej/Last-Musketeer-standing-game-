- 2026-10-18 — Turns are 1-based; `TurnResolver.ResolveTurn` advances `GameState.Turn` after its ticks (TurnStart.Begin then runs for the new turn) — one owner for the turn counter; initiative and kill credit read it.
- 2026-10-18 — ResolveTurn stops resolving ticks as soon as the round ends — nothing should happen after the last musketeer stands.
- 2026-10-18 — RevealSeconds 1.5 is stored as `RevealMs = 1500` — the sim is integer-only.
- 2026-10-18 — Costs are paid in Phase 0 (Fire unloads there too), so an action cancelled later (dash into a pillar, dash over a unit) keeps its cost; only Phase 0 fizzles are free — "fizzle = free" in §5 refers to the Phase 0 check.
- 2026-10-18 — Dash: a stationary unit on the landing tile blocks the whole dash (stays on its start tile, §6 2.3); only a pillar/crate/keg on the second tile shortens it to the first — literal reading of §6 2.1 vs 2.3.
- 2026-10-18 — ~~Dash crossing paths with another mover passes through it~~ — superseded by the crossing-paths bonk rule (Day 2, below).
- 2026-10-18 — Phase 2 movement bonks set no kill credit, and a unit bonks at most once per tick (1 damage) even if it's in a same-tile group and a swap — collisions are mutual accidents; dying from one is a self-kill unless someone hit you this/last turn.
- 2026-10-18 — Two or more movers wanting the same pit/off-board tile bonk like any same-tile collision and nobody falls — same rule everywhere is simplest.
- 2026-10-18 — A 3-unit rotation can't occur on a 4-direction grid (bipartite + dashes can't pass a unit in the middle tile); the smallest rotation is a 2×2 square of 4, which is what the test pins.
- 2026-10-18 — Pushes on one target combine as a vector sum: opposite pushes subtract (equal ones cancel), same direction adds (cap 3), both axes non-zero = Squeeze — one rule covers §6 3.3's three cases and mixed ones (e.g. N+S+E = pushed E 1).
- 2026-10-18 — Same-direction stacking is unreachable with plain shoves (only one unit fits behind a target); it's implemented and tested on PushMath for later push sources.
- 2026-10-18 — Push kill credit: first shover (initiative order) pushing in the net direction; for a squeeze the first shover overall; a fully cancelled push changes nothing and gives no credit. A unit bonked by a pushed unit is also credited to that shover.
- 2026-10-18 — Guard Break cancels the parry whenever a parrying unit is shoved at all (even if the pushes cancel or squeeze) — "Shove beats Parry".
- 2026-10-18 — Crates (and pillars, empty tiles, pits) can't be shoved; the Shoved event still fires so playback can show the whiff — §6 Phase 3 only pushes units and kegs.
- 2026-10-18 — Keg kicks resolve after all unit pushes, in keg id order (independent of initiative); several kicks on one keg combine like pushes (direction only); a kicked keg stops before pillars, crates, kegs and units — units settle first, then the kegs slide.
- 2026-10-18 — Shots on one snapshot: a crate or keg hit this tick still blocks other shots this tick, a crate is destroyed once, and a shot-hit keg is only marked `Detonating` until Phase 5 lands on Day 2.
- 2026-10-18 — Several shots hitting one unit in a tick: all damage lands; kill credit goes to the first shooter in initiative order (reflections count in the order of the shot they came from) — deterministic, no "simultaneous last attacker" ambiguity.
- 2026-10-18 — A reflected shot meeting a parrying unit is blocked (no damage) but not reflected again; in practice a reflection always meets the original shooter first, so this only matters for future abilities.
- 2026-10-18 — "Lethal damage in Phase 2 still shoots in Phase 4": Phase 2 damage only hits movers, who can't also fire, so the reachable case is lethal Phase 3 damage (wall slam/bonk/squeeze) on a unit that fires; that's what the Double KO test pins.
- 2026-10-18 — Units at 0 HP stay on the board (block, can be shoved and shot) until Phase 6; HP is clamped at 0; fallen units are removed instantly and stop acting.
- 2026-10-18 — Self-kill is `Died.Other == -1`; kill credit window = `KillCreditTurns = 1` (this turn or last).
- 2026-10-18 — Bonk is logged once per participant in both Phase 2 and Phase 3 (pushed unit and bystander each get one) — awards and the announcer count it the same way everywhere.
- 2026-10-18 — Added `SimEventKind.ParryRaised` (Phase 0, on a paid parry) — Parrying is cleared at tick end, so playback needs an event to show the guard.
- 2026-10-18 — TurnResolver throws if two plans claim the same slot; empty plans must be `new TurnPlan(slot)` — a `default(TurnPlan)` is slot 0 and would silently overwrite that player's plan.
- 2026-10-18 — **Rule change (Matej):** crossing paths bonk like a swap (§6 Phase 2.2). Implemented as: two movers bonk if any tile one enters is also entered by the other (first or second dash tile), or they traverse one edge in opposite directions; tiles they start on don't count, so following someone onto the tile they leave still works — one geometric rule covers dash-vs-dash, dash-vs-walker and perpendicular crossings.
- 2026-10-18 — **Rule change (Matej):** unplanned ticks are `ActionType.Idle` (0, the default), which does nothing and gives no Grit; Catch Breath only when planned (§5).
- 2026-10-18 — Disconnected players keep §9's "Catches Breath every tick": the host sends `TurnPlan.AllCatchBreath(slot)` for them (TECH_DESIGN §5 updated) — the Idle change is about unplanned ticks, not disconnects.
- 2026-10-18 — Keg target off the board or beyond Manhattan 3 fizzles (`FizzleReason.OutOfRange`), free; a target at distance 0 (your own tile) is legal and impacts on you — off-board would otherwise be an explosion centered outside the map.
- 2026-10-18 — Kegs land in thrower initiative order; a second keg landing on a tile where one just landed impacts it and both blow — deterministic and matches "a keg on the tile = not empty".
- 2026-10-18 — A keg that landed this tick skips this tick's fuse decrement, so it explodes on the same tick slot next turn (§5); scenario/map kegs count down from their first tick.
- 2026-10-18 — A thrower who fell earlier in the tick never throws (Grit still spent in Phase 0), like a fallen shooter never fires.
- 2026-10-18 — All explosions of a tick resolve together: the chain set is closed first (any keg in an exploding keg's 3×3, using positions before any push), then damage (max 1 per unit), crates, kegs removed, then pushes — one snapshot, like every other phase.
- 2026-10-18 — Keg kill credit: shot-detonated → first shooter in initiative order; fuse/impact → owner (-1 for map kegs = no credit); chain-detonated → inherits the credit of the keg that set it off — "I shot the keg that blew yours" counts.
- 2026-10-18 — Several explosion pushes on one unit combine like shoves (opposite cancel, perpendicular = Squeeze for 1 damage), applied in target initiative order, with wall slam/bonk/fall as in Phase 3 — "pushes follow Phase 3 push rules".
- 2026-10-18 — A parried explosion logs `Parried` with `Keg` set; MatchStats only counts parries with `Keg == -1` as "shots parried".
- 2026-10-18 — Grappin: whatever lands on a pit falls (a unit yanked onto the pit next to you dies with your credit; a keg is lost; a self-pull ending on a pit drops you) — "passes over pits safely" covers the tiles crossed, not the landing tile.
- 2026-10-18 — Grappin yank sets kill credit even when the target was already adjacent; a miss logs `Grappled` with Reason Miss so playback can draw the hook.
- 2026-10-18 — Switcheroo swaps with kegs too (no credit for a keg swap); a miss still spends the 3 Grit.
- 2026-10-18 — Bull Rush pushes/kicks only when the blocker was met within its 3 steps; running out of distance next to a unit doesn't count. The 2-tile push isn't a shove, so it neither Guard Breaks nor cancels a parry; the rusher gets the credit.
- 2026-10-18 — Double Charge "pierces up to 2 units" = hits at most 2 units and stops at the 2nd; a keg is detonated and stops it like a musket shot (§4 only says it stops at pillars and crates; kegs follow the Fire rule). `ShotFired.Reason = ShotKind.DoubleCharge`, `DamageKind.DoubleCharge`.
- 2026-10-18 — Signature costs are checked Grit first, then (Double Charge) the load; a Double Charge pays both in Phase 0.
- 2026-10-18 — Crumble is derived from the turn number (`Crumble.WarnedRing/FallingRing`): ring r warned at CrumbleStartTurn + r·CrumbleEvery, falls the next turn; everything on a fallen ring becomes pit (pillars and crates too) and kegs on it are lost — no extra GameState fields to sync.
- 2026-10-18 — If the crumble leaves ≤1 alive, the round ends at turn start (`RoundEnded` in the TurnStart log) — a round can't wait for a tick nobody can play.
- 2026-10-18 — On the 9×9 Courtyard the "center falls on turn 14 → draw" case can't be reached: ring 3 falls on 12, leaving one tile, so the round already ended; the rule is implemented generically and pinned on a custom config.
- 2026-10-18 — Every player keeps their slot, spawn and unit every round; a player sitting out a sudden-death round is spawned dead — Units stay indexed by player slot, so plans, stats and the network never need remapping.
- 2026-10-18 — Match end at round end: highest points ≥ PointsToWin wins outright (13 beats 12); a tie on points goes to most round wins; still tied → sudden-death round(s) among exactly those players, whose winner takes the match (a drawn tiebreak is replayed). Points from a sudden-death round still count on the scoreboard.
- 2026-10-18 — Points are collected during the round (kills as they happen, +3 at the end) and added to the totals at round end; the Crown is decided at round start from those totals (no Crown in round 1 or on any tie, incl. in sudden death).
- 2026-10-18 — "Swan Dive" (MatchStats.SelfRingOuts) counts every fall with no kill credit, including crumble falls; ripostes are counted separately from Sharpshooter shot hits.
- 2026-10-18 — `MatchRunner.PlayTurn` returns logs in playback order: Turn, then next TurnStart (if the round goes on), RoundResult + the finished GameState, then NextRoundStart — Day 3 presentation plays them in that order.
- 2026-10-18 — Bots own their DeterministicRng and a small memory (who stood still last turn) and never touch GameState.Rng — only the host plans bots, so their randomness must stay out of the shared state hash.

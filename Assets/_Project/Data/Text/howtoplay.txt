Every turn, everyone plans 3 actions in secret. When the timer ends (or all of you lock in), all plans play out at the same moment, one tick at a time.

Last musketeer standing wins the round. Points decide the match, and whoever wears the Crown becomes the target. Grit pays for the strong moves, and Catch Breath earns you more.

Nobody can walk off the map. On the Bridge, a push over an edge or into a missing corner is a deadly fall. On the Courtyard, the edges are walls. Crates break when shot, shoved or blown up, and may give +1 Grit. Kill points count the moment they happen, and a gold border means match point: if nobody overtakes you by the end of the round, you win.

Grappin (Matej, 5 Grit): hook a musketeer and drag them toward you. If they're dragged across a pit or water, they fall in. Hit a pillar, wall or crate and you pull yourself to it. Bull Rush (Nik, 5 Grit): charge up to 3 tiles. Whoever you hit takes 1 damage and flies back 5 tiles.
